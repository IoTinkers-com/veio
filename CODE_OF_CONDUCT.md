# Code of Conduct — VEIO

## Our standard
Be technical, honest and respectful. This project documents energy infrastructure with evidence; the same discipline applies to how we treat people.

## Expected behavior
- Argue from evidence; separate observations from interpretations — about data and about people.
- Respect that Venezuela's energy context is politically and socially sensitive; keep discussions technical and descriptive.
- Accept corrections gracefully; correct errors visibly (dated amendments), never silently.
- Credit others' work: datasets, methods, reviews.

## Unacceptable behavior
- Accusations or attribution of responsibility without evidence.
- Harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, or doxxing of any kind.
- Publishing sensitive infrastructure locations or personal data.
- Knowingly contributing fabricated data, invented sources or false licenses.
- Spam, advertising, or off-topic promotion.

## Enforcement
Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions that violate this code; repeated or severe violations lead to bans. Decisions are documented (state + reason), consistent with the contribution history policy. Reports: contact the maintainers privately (see `SECURITY.md` for the private channel) or via GitHub report-content tools.
